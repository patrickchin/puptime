Puptime is a private, offline puppy routine tracker built for quick logging. Record what happened in one tap, then see your puppy's day and patterns without creating an account.

• Log pee, poop, meals, potty trips, walks, naps, and custom activities
• Start and stop timed naps without inventing an end time
• Add notes by typing or optional voice dictation
• Edit dates and times when you need to correct a log
• Plan an editable daily routine with optional local reminders
• Review history, timing patterns, frequency summaries, and routine suggestions
• Log common activities from the iOS home-screen widget
• Export your complete activity history as a CSV file
• Use automatic light and dark themes

Your activity data stays on your device. Puptime has no account, subscription, ads, analytics, or tracking.

The starter routine is an editable example, not veterinary guidance. Adjust it to fit your puppy and your veterinarian's advice.
