Tap Pee, Poop, Meal, Walk, Nap, or another activity to record it; edit the time, type or dictate a note, or change the details later. Start and stop naps to track their duration.

Set a schedule if you need one, with or without reminders. History includes timing patterns and suggested logs, and can be exported as a CSV file. Choose 2–4 activities for the Home Screen widget.

Puptime does not require an account and has no ads, subscription, analytics, or tracking. Activity data stays on your device. Voice dictation uses Apple's speech-recognition service and may require a network connection.

The starter routine is an editable example, not veterinary advice. Adjust it with your veterinarian's guidance.
