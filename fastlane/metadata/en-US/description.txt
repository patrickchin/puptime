Set a schedule if you need one, with or without reminders. Compare timing patterns in Timeline, where possible gaps appear as faded marks without being added to history. Saved or imported estimated entries keep their Estimated label and are counted separately from recorded activity. Choose 2–4 Home Screen widget actions, including walks, naps, or custom activities. Start and stop naps from the widget to track their duration.

Export an activity CSV to keep a backup, then import it to recover missing entries while keeping your current edits. Backups include activity times, notes, custom activity names, nap state, and estimate labels. Routines and settings are not included.

Puptime tracks one pet without an account, ads, a subscription, analytics, or tracking. Activity data stays on your device unless you choose to export it. Voice dictation uses your device's speech-recognition service and may require a network connection.

The starter routine is an editable example, not veterinary advice. Adjust it with your veterinarian's guidance.
