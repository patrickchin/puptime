Know when your puppy last peed, pooped, or ate. Puptime keeps the little details in one place while you learn your puppy’s routine.

LOG IT WHILE IT HAPPENS
Tap Pee, Poop, or Eat to save an activity. Adjust the time if you’re catching up later, and add a note when the details matter. Use the Home Screen widget for quick logging.

FIND YOUR PUPPY’S PATTERN
Look back through your history and compare activity on the timeline. Insights helps you see how often your puppy goes and when. Estimated entries stay labelled and count separately from recorded activity.

MAKE THE ROUTINE YOURS
Edit your puppy’s routine and choose reminders that suit your day. Track walks and custom activities alongside potty breaks and meals.

START WITH LOCAL LOGGING
Local features are free and work offline without an account. Optional cloud backup and device sync require sign-in and cloud access. Puppy data is uploaded only after you choose to enable cloud access. Available plans and any trial are shown before you choose one.

KEEP A COPY OF YOUR HISTORY
Export your activities as a CSV, or import a backup to recover missing entries while keeping your current edits. Activity CSVs include times, notes, custom activity names, and estimate labels. Routines and settings are not included.

Voice dictation uses your device’s speech-recognition service and may need an internet connection. The starter routine is an editable example. Adjust it with your veterinarian’s guidance.
