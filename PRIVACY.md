# Puptime Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Puptime is a private, offline puppy routine tracker developed by Patrick Chin. This policy explains how the app handles information.

## Information handling

Puptime does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or a Puptime-operated server. Activity logs, notes, and routine settings are stored locally on your device. Puptime does not collect or transmit that information to the developer.

## Speech recognition

If you choose to dictate a note, Puptime asks for microphone and speech-recognition permission and uses Apple's speech-recognition service to turn your speech into text. Puptime does not store the audio recording. Speech processing is provided by Apple and may require a network connection depending on your device and downloaded language models. The resulting text is stored locally as part of your note.

## Notifications

If you enable routine reminders, Puptime schedules notifications locally on your device. It does not register your device with a Puptime notification server.

## Exports and sharing

Puptime only exports activity data when you choose **Export activity CSV**. The file is created in temporary app storage and passed to the iOS share sheet. Puptime does not control the app or destination you select for sharing.

## Retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete individual records or remove the app. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data, subject to your device backup settings.

## Changes

If Puptime's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the relevant app release.

## Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the [Puptime support page](https://github.com/patrickchin/puptime/issues).
