# Puptime marketing context

Store/trial guidance clarified November 14, 2026. Read `docs/product-operating-plan.md` for current release gates and operating decisions. A GitHub recovery APK is not a store launch; later merged UI work is not necessarily in that APK.

Puptime is a native puppy activity log. Local logging stays free without an account. Optional accounts, cloud backup/device sync and sharing require release qualification; planned paid cloud services are a later phase. Do not market development access as a paid or permanently free production offer.

The first audience is English-speaking households in the first weeks of puppy toilet training. Initial acquisition is a small Android beta through existing relationships and reward-based trainers/puppy classes. Geography remains a hypothesis to validate with the first suitable households; do not invent country-specific availability.

The core job is quick recording of potty breaks and meals, clear last-log history, and a routine the owner can adjust. Proposed promise: **A free puppy log for the first weeks at home.** Supporting message: **See when your puppy last went, and how their routine is taking shape.** Widgets, reminders, sharing and local storage are not unique market claims.

Keep the name **Puptime**; proposed store display name **Puptime: Puppy Potty Log**. Prefer **getpuptime.com**, with **usepuptime.com** as a fallback, subject to current registrar and name-conflict checks. No domain purchase or trademark clearance has been completed. The current site is `https://puptime.pages.dev`.

The owner has little time. The root chat coordinates routine priorities and recommendations; assigned agents implement, test, build and deliver PRs. Codex prepares reviewable assets and exact install/hold advice. Default marketing spend is zero. Use one qualified beta destination, the approved screenshots and roughly 15-second native preview in PR #157, trainer/class introductions, up to two posts a week and improvements to three existing high-intent care guides. Reuse that media package after candidate, asset-rights and store checks; do not commission a duplicate demo. Avoid a daily multi-channel founder content obligation.

Copy should be plain, specific and warm. Use verified product behavior and real feedback with consent. Do not invent testimonials, clinical outcomes, prediction accuracy, retention or market demand. Keep diagnostics, raw exports and personal usage analysis private. The owner specifically approved PR #157's screenshots/native preview using their history for store/launch media; this approval applies only to that package and does not authorize publishing other history or support reports.

Prepare outreach/social/listing drafts for review. Do not send, post, purchase, submit or announce without the action's explicit session authorization. Do not advertise in communities that prohibit promotion, including r/puppy101. The proposed five-household product trial and activation targets are optional learning suggestions, not owner-agreed release gates. Keep any applicable store-mandated testing separate; see the current plan and store-readiness checklist.
