# Puptime marketing context

Updated November 12, 2026. Read `docs/product-operating-plan.md` for current release gates and operating decisions.

Puptime is a native puppy activity log. Local logging stays free without an account. Optional accounts, cloud backup/device sync and sharing require release qualification; planned paid cloud services are a later phase. Do not market development access as a paid or permanently free production offer.

The first audience is English-speaking households in the first weeks of puppy toilet training. Initial acquisition is a small Android beta through existing relationships and reward-based trainers/puppy classes. Geography remains a hypothesis to validate with the first suitable households; do not invent country-specific availability.

The core job is quick recording of potty breaks and meals, clear last-log history, and a routine the owner can adjust. Proposed promise: **A free puppy log for the first weeks at home.** Supporting message: **See when your puppy last went, and how their routine is taking shape.** Widgets, reminders, sharing and local storage are not unique market claims.

Keep the name **Puptime**; proposed store display name **Puptime: Puppy Potty Log**. Prefer **getpuptime.com**, with **usepuptime.com** as a fallback, subject to current registrar and name-conflict checks. No domain purchase or trademark clearance has been completed. The current site is `https://puptime.pages.dev`.

The owner has little time. Codex chooses routine priorities, prepares reviewable assets and gives exact install/hold advice. Default marketing spend is zero. Use one qualified beta destination, a reusable short demo, trainer/class introductions, up to two posts a week and improvements to three existing high-intent care guides. Avoid a daily multi-channel founder content obligation.

Copy should be plain, specific and warm. Use verified product behavior and real feedback with consent. Do not invent testimonials, clinical outcomes, prediction accuracy, retention or market demand. Personal usage evidence and private exports never belong in public marketing, repository files or PRs.

Prepare outreach/social/listing drafts for review. Do not send, post, purchase, submit or announce without the action's explicit session authorization. Do not advertise in communities that prohibit promotion, including r/puppy101. See the plan for independent-household beta gates; treat them as provisional decision thresholds rather than external benchmarks.
